# Febland — furniture, lighting, artwork & gifts

> ✅ = confirmed · ❓ = needs input. Detailed sales rules: `../sales-factory/brain/`.

## Who we are ✅
Family-run in Blackpool since **1952**. We import modern furniture, lighting and interior
accessories (Italy & China) **and** design/print our own **custom velvet** furniture and artwork
in-house — the differentiator no importer can match. ~5,000 SKUs; 500+ UK trade stockists.

## Two sides of the business ✅
- **Trade** (trade.febland.co.uk, WooCommerce) — most trade sales; wholesale to retailers,
  designers, hospitality. **This is the growth priority (per Dexter).**
- **Retail** (febland.co.uk, Shopify) — direct to consumer; also on Amazon, eBay, Wayfair, Faire.

## What we offer ✅
Furniture, lighting, wall mirrors, mosaic lamps, chrome/ceramics, bar & contract furniture, wall art,
home accessories, gifts — plus the bespoke velvet range (the wedge).

## Who our customers are ✅ _(see sales-factory/prospecting for detail)_
Independent furniture retailers · interior designers · lifestyle/gift boutiques · hospitality &
contract FF&E · home stagers/holiday-let furnishers.

## Channels ✅
Trade: WooCommerce, Faire. Retail: Shopify, Amazon, eBay, Wayfair. Social (Glen's remit): drive
retail + showroom. All order feeds wire into the revenue tiles (`engine/src/feeds/`).
