## Who we are ✅
A family-run group based in **Blackpool** (Ashworth Road, FY4 4UN — at the end of the M55). Built
around **Febland**, the furniture/lighting/artwork/gifts business trading since 1952, the group now
spans four businesses on and around our own land.

## Why they belong together (the synergies the agents should exploit)
- **Upholstery ↔ Febland:** Febland sells contract furniture to hotels/bars/restaurants;
  Blackpool Upholstery re-upholsters and makes bespoke seating for the *same* buyers. One hospitality
  lead can feed both. The custom velvet is the shared crown jewel.
- **Storage ↔ everyone:** local businesses, house-movers, and Febland's own trade customers all need
  storage. Cross-sell and local-SEO opportunity.
- **Rentals ↔ local trade:** commercial tenants are local businesses — a warm audience for the others.
