# 02 · Qualification — how we decide to pursue

> **AI-drafted v0.1.** ✅ = draft, confirm/edit · ❓ = need your input

## Must-have signals before we spend effort ✅ _(draft)_
- Sells home / furniture / interiors / gifts (not unrelated)
- Has a trade premises, showroom, website, or live projects
- Not a competitor or another wholesaler
- Reachable (verified contact for a buyer/owner)

## Positive signals that raise priority ✅ _(draft)_
- Premium/"distinctive/bespoke/statement" positioning on their site
- Boutique-hotel, designer, or multi-site work
- Actively posting new arrivals / clearly trading and growing
- Curated, design-led range (fits the velvet wedge)

## Disqualifiers (auto-drop / suppress) ✅ _(draft — confirm)_
- Competitor / wholesaler · dormant or closed business · pure clearance/discount model
- Already an existing account (dedupe vs Shopify + Woo + the 500) · opted out

## What "ready to buy" looks like ✅ _(draft)_
Asks for a line sheet / catalogue · asks about MOQ or trade terms · mentions a specific project or
room · requests samples · asks about the custom velvet. → escalate warmth, move toward onboarding.
