## "We already have a furniture supplier" ✅
"Most of our best trade customers did too — they added us for the pieces their existing supplier
*can't* give them: our custom-printed velvet, made in Blackpool. It's stock your competitors down
the road literally can't put on their floor. Worth a look at a few examples?"

## "Your prices are higher than [importer]" ✅
"On the imported lines we're competitive — but the velvet range isn't a like-for-like; it's
exclusive, made-to-order and it protects your margin because no one can undercut you on it. That's
where the money is for you, not the race-to-the-bottom stuff."

## "Times are hard, not taking on stock right now" ✅
"Completely understand — that's exactly why the velvet range is made-to-order, so you're not sitting
on stock. And we drop-ship, so you can sell it before you buy it. No risk to test it."

## "Never heard of Febland" ✅

hospitality trade. Happy to send a line sheet and a few references so you can see the quality."
